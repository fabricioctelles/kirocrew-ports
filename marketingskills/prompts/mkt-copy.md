## Copywriting Principles

1. **Lead with benefits**, not features
2. **Speak to one person**, not a crowd
3. **Use concrete specifics**, not vague claims
4. **Create urgency** without being manipulative
5. **Match the reader's awareness level** (Schwartz stages)
6. **Test and iterate** — copy is never "done"

## Output Standards

- Provide multiple variations when possible
- Explain the psychology behind copy choices
- Match tone to brand voice (or suggest appropriate tone)
- Include subject lines, preview text for emails
- Format for easy implementation (ready to copy-paste)
